Building the Future of Data Infrastructure

Rivercity Internet Group has been at the forefront of internet infrastructure since the early days of the .com boom. What started as a mission to power fantasy sports platforms and help major brands establish their online presence has evolved into a legacy of building and operating some of the most sophisticated data center facilities in the world.

Today, we combine decades of operational excellence with cutting-edge innovation in liquid immersion cooling, edge computing, and AI infrastructure. We don’t just adapt to the future—we build it.

Our Journey

Late 1990sThe Beginning

Founded during the .com era, RCIG and Hostirian emerged as pioneers in internet hosting and infrastructure. We powered fantasy sports platforms and helped major brands establish their digital presence, building the foundation for decades of innovation.

2000sExpansion & Expertise

As the internet matured, so did we. We expanded our colocation and hosting services, developing deep expertise in data center operations, network architecture, and high-availability infrastructure that would define industry standards.

2010sEnterprise-Scale Infrastructure

RCIG transitioned into building enterprise-scale data centers, including facilities for some of the world's largest coal companies. These massive projects demonstrated our capability to design, construct, and operate mission-critical infrastructure at any scale.

2020sThe AI Revolution

Recognizing the transformative power of artificial intelligence, we pivoted to develop next-generation infrastructure solutions. We pioneered liquid immersion cooling technology for high-density compute and began architecting edge and WAN-scale AI infrastructure.

What Drives Us

Builder Mentality

We don't just operate infrastructure—we build it from the ground up. Our team has designed and constructed data centers from concept to completion, giving us unmatched insight into every layer of the stack.

Innovation First

From liquid immersion cooling to WAN-scale AI infrastructure, we invest in technologies that will define the next decade. We're not waiting for the future—we're engineering it.

Proven Partnership

25+ years of operations means we've weathered every technological shift and market change. Our clients trust us because we've been here before, and we'll be here for the long haul.

Sustainable by Design

Liquid immersion cooling isn't just more efficient—it's a paradigm shift in how we think about energy use. We're committed to building infrastructure that's both powerful and responsible.

Edge-First Vision

The future isn't centralized megafacilities—it's distributed, community-scale data centers closer to where computation happens. We're architecting the edge infrastructure that will power real-time AI.

Always-On Excellence

Uptime isn't a metric—it's a commitment. From our earliest hosting days to today's AI workloads, we've built a culture of operational excellence that never sleeps.

Where We're Headed

The next chapter of internet infrastructure isn’t about bigger—it’s about smarter, faster, and closer. We’re building distributed AI infrastructure that brings computation to the edge, enabling real-time intelligence where it’s needed most.

Our vision for WAN AI FTEs (WANAIE) represents a fundamental shift in how businesses deploy AI. Instead of cloud-dependent models, we’re creating always-on AI employees that run continuously at distributed edge locations, providing instant intelligence without latency, bandwidth constraints, or privacy concerns.
