Episode #110: Ironwood Wellness – Kerry Streach

Kerry Steach of Ironwood Wellness: Turning Pain into Purpose and Building a Wellness Community in St. Louis Kerry Steach, founder and owner of Ironwood Wellness, brings a personal story of resilience to the world of fitness and wellness coaching. After overcoming debilitating chronic back pain, Kerry built a business focused on helping others move better, feel better, and live with energy. His leadership mindset and practical approach to combining in-person and virtual training make him a standout in the fitness entrepreneur space. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j9Ctj5HNbmY&list=PL-aGDTmvrRSNUYOSG_x0JG7BNLmjoLWZs&index=45 This Episode’s Highlights Kerry’s personal struggle with chronic back pain inspired his career shift from corporate banking to wellness coaching. Ironwood Wellness offers both in-person training at its Webster Groves location and virtual sessions for clients anywhere. Leadership, according to Kerry, is all about “walking the talk” and setting an example for your team and clients. The COVID-19 pandemic taught Kerry the value of hybrid fitness models, blending technology with human connection. He believes the future of wellness relies on getting people “out of the box”—meaning more movement, less screen time.
